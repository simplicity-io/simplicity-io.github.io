Privacy policy: Ultimate Platform Inspector

Ultimate Platform Inspector (package io.simplicity.loupe), published by Simplicity.IO. Last updated: 27 November 2026. 繁體中文

Data that stays on your phone

The app shows information about your phone (system, hardware, sensors, battery, connectivity, media codecs and features) on the device only. It has no account and no sign-in. Nothing you see in the app is sent anywhere. An export is started by you and goes where you choose.

Data collected by third-party services

  • Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase), release builds only, collects pseudonymous usage data: screens viewed and features used, rewarded unlocks, and your device model and approximate country. It also records where the install came from: our own app slug, “play_other” or “none”; the raw referrer string is not stored. Firebase keeps a random ID per install, so the data is pseudonymous, not anonymous. There is no user ID, and the Advertising ID and Android ID are not used for analytics. Google Signals is off. The data is not sold and not used for advertising.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends crash reports (release builds only).
  • Google AdMob shows ads (a native card, a banner, and rewarded ads that unlock export and extra widget slots). AdMob may use the Advertising ID, and shows personalised ads only where you have consented. See Google's How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Firebase Remote Config fetches switches for the ads, from Google servers.

Retention: Analytics event data is kept for 2 months and user data for 14 months, reset when the app is used again. The BigQuery export is kept for 60 days.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Google's consent form (UMP) appears before any ad request. Analytics storage follows your choice: it stays denied until you answer, and Google then receives cookieless, modelled data only. Open “Privacy settings” in the app to change your choice.

What I do not do

I do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location or files, I do not sell personal data, and I do not use analytics data for advertising.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
