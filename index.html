Mainline Module Info

Shows which Google Play system update (Mainline) modules your phone has, their versions, and when each one last changed. No root, no account; the history stays on your phone.

Linux Terminal Helper

Android 15 and up ships a Linux Terminal inside the Virtualization module, switched off. This walks you through turning it on, opens it, and adds a home-screen shortcut.

  • Android 15+ (API 35)
  • needs Android's built-in Linux Terminal
