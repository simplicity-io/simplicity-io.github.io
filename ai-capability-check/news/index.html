OnDevice AI Meter: what changed

Changes to how phones are tested and scored, and to which phones are supported, newest first. Scores are compared only within the same suite version (see the methodology, "Fairness rules and versioning").

2026-11-20

  • AI multitasking test: steadier method. It keeps one fast CPU core free to feed the AI, measures the other work again before every step, and tries a failed step once more (the better try counts).

2026-11-18: suite 5

  • The chat test runs Gemma 3 1B on every phone instead of Qwen3 1.7B, so chat results compare across chips.
  • The efficiency test becomes the AI multitasking test: how much CPU work the AI processor saves, and how many camera-like AI jobs it keeps on time beside other work. Not part of the score.

2026-11-17

  • The methodology explains why the CPU is the reference and what the score doesn't show.

2026-11-15

  • An efficiency test replaces the heat test: it shows how much CPU work the AI or graphics processor saves. It is not part of the score.
  • The chat quiz asks about the days of the week instead of the planets, which the small model didn't know well enough for a fair test.

2026-11-14: suite 4

  • The CPU test uses all fast cores (4 to 8) instead of 4 on every phone.

2026-11-13

  • The score is the sum of the test scores. Earlier results were recalculated.

2026-11-12: suite 3

  • Image style counts in the score.
  • Results measured while charging are marked "Charging".

2026-11-05: suite 2

  • The score has two halves, Recognition AI and Generative AI.
  • Sound recognition is scored.
