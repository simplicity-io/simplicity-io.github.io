Privacy policy: OnDevice AI Meter

OnDevice AI Meter (formerly AI Capability Check; package io.simplicity.benchpress), published by Simplicity.IO. Last updated: 12 November 2026.

Data that stays on your phone

The app runs AI tests on your phone and saves each run's results and technical log on your phone, in the app's own storage. They are not uploaded. The test photos and sounds ship with the app; the app does not use your camera, microphone, photos or files.

Model downloads

The chat and speech-to-text tests need AI models that are downloaded only when you tap Download. They come from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) or, if Hugging Face is unavailable, from an alternative address the app is configured with. Like any download, the server sees your device's IP address. Each file is checked against a fixed fingerprint (SHA-256) before it is used.

Data collected by third-party services

  • Firebase Analytics collects anonymous usage data: when a test run starts, finishes, fails or is interrupted (and why, for example low memory), the scores and level, which tests finished, and technical device information (phone model, chip, graphics processor, memory size, Android and app version). It does not include your name, account or any content.
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports. If you tap "Report a problem", it also receives a short technical report (phone model, chip, graphics driver, which tests failed); nothing is sent unless you tap Send.
  • Firebase Remote Config fetches app settings (for example, download addresses) from Google servers.
  • Google Play Install Referrer tells the app whether it was installed from a link in another Simplicity.IO app; only the name of that app is recorded.
  • Google AdMob shows ads in three places: under your result after a test run has finished, on the test history and run details pages, and on the downloads page. Ads never load or show while a test runs. Before the first ad, Google's consent form asks for your choices where the law requires it; you can change them any time under "Privacy settings" in the menu (shown where it applies). AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and IP address to show and measure ads, as described in Google's policies.

These services are operated by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Internet: model downloads and the services above.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and later, optional): shows download progress.
  • Foreground service and keeping the device awake: let a model download continue when you leave the app.
  • Seeing other apps: checks only whether Simplicity.IO's other apps, and Android's Linux Terminal, are installed, to show "Open" or "Get it on Google Play" on the More apps page.

What I do not do

I do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, recordings or files, and I do not sell personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
