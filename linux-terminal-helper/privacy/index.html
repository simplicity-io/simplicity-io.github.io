Privacy policy

Linux Terminal Helper (package io.simplicity.toybox), published by Simplicity.IO. This policy applies from version 0.6.19 (build 29). Last updated: 27 November 2026.

Data that stays on your device

The app checks whether your phone includes Android's Linux Terminal and guides you through setup. The Terminal itself runs on your device; the app does not upload its contents.

Data collected by third-party services

  • Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase), in release builds only, collects pseudonymous usage data: app usage such as screens viewed and button taps, your device model and approximate country. It is used to help improve the app. Firebase keeps a random ID per install, so the data is pseudonymous, not anonymous. There is no user ID, and Analytics does not collect your Advertising ID or Android ID. Ad personalization signals and Google Signals are off. The data is not sold and not used for ads.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends crash reports and the device model in release builds only.
  • Google AdMob shows banner ads and may use the Advertising ID. Where consent is required, Google's consent form (UMP) appears first, and a “Privacy settings” item in the app menu lets you change your choice.

Analytics runs whether or not you give consent for ads. It is separate from ads.

Retention: Analytics event data is kept for 2 months and user data for 14 months, reset when the app is used again. The BigQuery export is kept for 60 days.

These services are operated by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Google Consent Mode is used. Analytics storage is denied until you agree in Google's consent form, which is the same form used for ads. Before that, Google receives only cookieless, modelled data. Open “Privacy settings” in the app to change your choice.

What I do not do

I do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location or files, I do not sell personal data, and I do not use analytics data for advertising.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
