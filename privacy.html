Privacy policy

Mainline Module Info (package io.simplicity.trainspotting), published by Simplicity.IO. Last updated: 27 November 2026.

Data that stays on your device

The app reads the list of installed system modules and their versions to display them. The module update history is stored in a local database on your device, and the history itself is not uploaded. Pseudonymous analytics events (see below) can include aggregate information about it, such as how many module versions changed in a scan and the package name of a module that was rolled back.

Data collected by third-party services

  • Google AdMob shows ads and may use an advertising ID and device information. In regions where consent is required, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice in Settings → Privacy Settings.
  • Firebase Analytics collects pseudonymous usage data (screens viewed, features used, app settings such as whether notifications are on), device model and approximate country, to help improve the app. It is linked only to a random ID that Firebase creates per install, not to your name or account. It is not sold and not used for ads, and Google Signals is off. Data is kept for 2 months for events and 14 months for user data (reset when the app is used again); the raw export is kept for 60 days. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears first and analytics storage follows your choice; you can change it under Privacy Settings.
  • Firebase Remote Config fetches app configuration values from Google servers.
  • Google Play services handle in-app review prompts.

These services are operated by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications: optional alerts when a module updates.
  • Receive boot completed: used only if you opt in to “Notify after reboot”.

What I do not do

I do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location or files, I do not sell personal data, and I do not use analytics data for advertising.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
