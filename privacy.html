Privacy policy

Mainline Module Info (package io.simplicity.trainspotting), published by Simplicity.IO. Last updated: 27 October 2026.

Data that stays on your device

The app reads the list of installed system modules and their versions to display them. The module update history is stored in a local database on your device, and the history itself is not uploaded. Anonymous analytics events (see below) can include aggregate information about it, such as how many module versions changed in a scan and the package name of a module that was rolled back.

Data collected by third-party services

  • Google AdMob shows ads and may use an advertising ID and device information. In regions where consent is required, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice in Settings → Privacy Settings.
  • Firebase Analytics collects anonymous usage data (screens viewed, features used, app settings such as whether notifications are on) and technical device information to help improve the app.
  • Firebase Remote Config fetches app configuration values from Google servers.
  • Google Play services handle in-app review prompts.

These services are operated by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications: optional alerts when a module updates.
  • Receive boot completed: used only if you opt in to “Notify after reboot”.

What I do not do

I do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or files, and I do not sell personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
